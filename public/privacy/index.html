Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Mohammed Gous Ali Shah (Kamel Shah). It collects what you choose to send through the contact form or the chat assistant, plus anonymous statistics about visits. It uses no cookies and no advertising trackers.

Contact form

When you send a message, the form collects:

  • your first and last name
  • your email address
  • your phone number, if you choose to add it
  • your message

These details are sent as an email to my inbox (kamelshah2003@gmail.com), with your email set as the reply-to address. I use them only to read and reply to your message. They are not added to a mailing list, sold or shared with anyone else, and they stay in my inbox until I delete them.

To block spam, the server briefly keeps your IP address in memory to limit how many messages can be sent in an hour. It is not written to a database and is discarded within an hour.

Chat assistant

The assistant answers questions about my work using information that is already published on this site. Your questions are sent to this site’s server to get an answer and are not saved. The conversation is cleared when you close or reload the page.

Visit statistics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not identify you. For each page view it records the page address, the site that referred you, your approximate location (country, region and city), and your device type, operating system and browser. Visits are grouped using a temporary code generated from your request, which is discarded after 24 hours. I only see totals, such as how many people viewed the projects section.

Hosting and fonts

  • Vercel hosts this site. Like any web host, it processes technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google’s servers. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Links to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, GitHub and X take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask me what information you have sent, or ask me to delete it, by emailing kamelshah2003@gmail.com. I will reply within a reasonable time.

Changes

If this site starts collecting anything new, this page will be updated first and the date above will change.
